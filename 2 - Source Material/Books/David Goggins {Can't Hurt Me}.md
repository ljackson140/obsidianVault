1. Ask yourself Tough Questions
	- if you don't ask them yourself, nobody will
	- examine yourself on a deep level
	- Get inside of your mind 
	**Warning:
	- Asking Tough questions 
	- ->WILL REQUIRE <-
	- Giving Tough Answers 
2. Give yourself **honest** answers
	- DONT LIE TO YOURSELF 
	- In a world full of lies and liars;
		- the least we can do is be honest with ourselves
	- Lying to yourself will only hurt you
	- Being honest with yourself will help you 
3. MAKE A PLAN
	- write it down 
	- know what success looks like for yourself 
	- set yourself up to be successful 
	- be conscience of how you're spending your time + effort
	- prepare yourself for the journey to come 
	- Something will go wrong -> have a "plan B"
4.  CALLOUS YOUR MIND 
	- Do things that make you uncomfortable 
	- sustainable changes require micro level actions 
	- consistently DO IT  (again, again and again)
	"The more often you're uncomfortable, the more you'll be able to control your self dialogue.
	In stressful situations and all areas of life". - That's a super power
5. REMOVE THE GOVERNOR
	- What's the governor?
	- The voice in your head that's always trying to convince you to take the easy way out:
		- "Eat out just this once and you can meal prep later"
		- "Since I don't want to get injured I wont run today"
		- "I have so much time to do my work"
		- "I'll do X later instead of now"
		- "One more chapter"
	- It knows you inside and out
	- it doesnt care if you're:
		- tired
		- stresses
		- obese
		- addicted
		- depressed
	- ALL it cares about is taking the path of least resistance 
	- Don't Be a Victim of It -> REMOVE IT
6. FALL IN LOVE WITH FAILURE 
	- Master the process of dissecting your failures 
	- Every failure can expand your playbook for life... if you let it
	- Stop trying to avoid it; instead use it 
		"The day we stop failing is the day we stop trying".
	- More action/repetition will result in ore failure 
	- The more we fail + keep going; the less value we give each failure 
	- SO FAIL...because failing is learning and learning is improving 
7. FIND YOUR "WHY"
	- Why are you doing this?
	- Why are you here?
	- Why does this matter to you?
	You need a reason (or a "why") for the hard tasks you attempt.
	Your "Why" needs to be something bigger than yourself 
	Without a solid "Why" you will surrender to your governor 
	Your "Why" can change according to the season of life you're in
	Each area of your life can/should have it's own "Why":
	- Physical "Why"
	- Spiritual "Why"
	- Occupational "Why"
	- Educational "Why"
